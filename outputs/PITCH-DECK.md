# 🌲 The Vision

**What if 20 families could spend a year living in the wilderness together?**

- Learning alongside their children
- Working remotely via Starlink
- Conducting real citizen science
- Building genuine, lasting community

A modern expedition combining NASA mission structure with wilderness immersion philosophy.

# 🧭 The Concept

## NASA Mission Structure
Every family has **primary and backup roles:**
- Medical Lead / Backup Medical
- Safety Officer / Backup Safety
- Water & Sanitation Lead
- Communications Lead
- Education Coordinator
- Logistics Coordinator
- Community Facilitator
- Youth Council Representative

## Modern Connectivity
- **Starlink** for remote work
- **Satellite phones** for emergency
- **Drone delivery** for medical resupply
- **Helicopter on call** for evacuation

## Scientific Purpose
- Partner with universities for citizen science
- Contribute to iNaturalist, eBird, phenology networks
- Publish research from year-long continuous dataset
- Children become real field researchers

---

# 🎯 Target Market

## "Hardcore Homeschool" Families

**Demographics:**
- Already homeschooling by choice
- Values-driven (nature, community, education)
- At least one remote-work capable adult
- Financial stability (can commit €60-85K/family)
- Prior wilderness/outdoor experience
- Children ages 5-17

**What They're Seeking:**
- Deep nature immersion (not just camping trips)
- Genuine community (not just neighbors)
- Educational excellence (not just unschooling)
- Character development through challenge
- Break from digital overwhelm

# 🗺️ Location Candidates

## Tier 1 (Researching First)

| Region | Key Advantages | Key Challenges |
|--------|----------------|----------------|
| **Scandinavia** | Freedom to roam, wilderness culture | Winter severity, cost |
| **Scotland** | Right to roam, English-speaking | Weather, planning permits |
| **New Zealand** | Strong outdoor culture, DOC land | Distance, permit process |
| **BC Canada** | Vast crown land, English-speaking | Bears, indigenous considerations |
| **Patagonia** | Epic wilderness, growing infrastructure | Distance, regulatory clarity |

## Selection Criteria
1. Clear permit pathway (25%)
2. Wilderness quality (15%)
3. Climate habitability (15%)
4. Water availability (10%)
5. Emergency access (10%)
6. Resupply logistics (10%)
7. Citizen science potential (5%)
8. Cost (5%)
9. Cultural accessibility (5%)

# 💰 Budget Structure

| Category | Low | Target | High |
|----------|-----|--------|------|
| Pre-Launch | €120K | €175K | €255K |
| Infrastructure | €200K | €290K | €425K |
| Operations | €700K | €850K | €1,020K |
| Contingency | €120K | €175K | €255K |
| **TOTAL** | **€1.14M** | **€1.49M** | **€1.96M** |

### Major Cost Drivers
- **Staffing** (20-30%): Expedition leaders, safety officers
- **Food** (15-20%): €10-15/person/day × 80 people × 365 days
- **Infrastructure** (20-25%): Shelters, water, sanitation, power
- **Logistics** (10-15%): Transport, resupply, equipment

### Revenue Sources
- Family fees (primary)
- Foundation grants (education, citizen science)
- Corporate sponsorship (outdoor brands)
- University research partnerships
