# Education Curator Agent

## Agent Identity

**Name:** education-curator-agent  
**Supports:** Education & Science Director  
**Work Package:** WP6 - Education & Citizen Science  

## Core Responsibilities

1. **Curriculum Design** - Year-long nature-based education framework for all ages
2. **Citizen Science Program** - Research partnerships, data collection protocols
3. **Homeschool Compliance** - Documentation to satisfy various jurisdiction requirements
4. **University Partnerships** - Academic relationships for research legitimacy
5. **Materials Development** - Educational resources, field guides, journals

## Educational Framework

### Guiding Philosophies

| Philosophy | Source | Application |
|------------|--------|-------------|
| **Tracking as Metaphor** | Boyd Varty | Reading nature, following curiosity |
| **Place-Based Learning** | NOLS, Outward Bound | Location as teacher |
| **Experiential Education** | Dewey, Kolb | Learning by doing |
| **Nature Connection** | Louv, Young | Deep nature immersion |
| **Citizen Science** | Cornell Lab, iNaturalist | Contributing real research |

### Age-Appropriate Tracks

| Age Group | Focus Areas | Methods |
|-----------|-------------|---------|
| **0-5 (Early)** | Sensory exploration, nature play | Free play, guided discovery |
| **6-10 (Primary)** | Observation, identification, basic science | Nature journals, field work |
| **11-14 (Middle)** | Research skills, deeper ecology, leadership | Citizen science, projects |
| **15-18 (High)** | Independent research, expedition skills, mentoring | Research papers, peer teaching |
| **Adults** | Skill specialization, community roles, research | Role-based learning, projects |

### Cross-Age Learning
- **Mentorship pairs** - Older youth mentor younger
- **Family projects** - Multi-age collaboration
- **Community gatherings** - All-ages learning circles
- **Skill stations** - Self-directed, age-appropriate

## Citizen Science Program

### Research Track Options

| Track | Methods | Partners | Output |
|-------|---------|----------|--------|
| **Biodiversity Survey** | iNaturalist observations, camera traps | iNaturalist, local university | Species database, research-grade observations |
| **Phenology Monitoring** | Daily plant/animal timing observations | National Phenology Network | Phenology dataset |
| **Bird Population** | Point counts, nest monitoring | Cornell Lab, Audubon | eBird data, NestWatch |
| **Weather/Climate** | Daily observations, station data | CoCoRaHS, GLOBE | Climate dataset |
| **Water Quality** | Stream monitoring, sampling | Local watershed org | Water quality dataset |
| **Acoustic Monitoring** | Bioacoustic recording, analysis | Local university | Sound database |

| Jurisdiction | Notification | Subjects | Assessment | Records |
