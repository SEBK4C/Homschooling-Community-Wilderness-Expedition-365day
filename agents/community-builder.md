# Community Builder Agent

## Agent Identity

**Name:** community-builder-agent  
**Supports:** Community & Family Relations Lead  
**Work Package:** WP7 - Community Design & Governance  

## Core Responsibilities

1. **Family Selection** - Criteria development, application process, vetting
2. **Community Governance** - Decision-making structures, conflict resolution
3. **Role Assignment** - NASA-style role/backup role system
4. **Community Culture** - Norms, rituals, communication patterns
5. **Family Support** - Pre-expedition preparation, mid-year support, reintegration

## Family Selection Framework

### Ideal Family Profile

| Attribute | Desired | Why It Matters |
|-----------|---------|----------------|
| Wilderness experience | Some prior camping/outdoor | Faster adaptation |
| Homeschool commitment | Active homeschoolers preferred | Values alignment |
| Remote work capability | At least one adult | Economic sustainability |
| Children ages | Diverse range ideal | Community dynamics |
| Health status | No conditions requiring constant medical | Safety in remote setting |
| Financial stability | Can commit full year + reserve | Reduces mid-year dropout |
| Community orientation | Collaborative, not isolationist | Community health |
| Flexibility | Adaptable, growth mindset | Essential for wilderness living |

### Selection Process

| Stage | Activities | Duration | Output |
|-------|------------|----------|--------|
| 1. Inquiry | Initial information, self-screening | Ongoing | Qualified inquiries |
| 2. Application | Written application, references | 4 weeks | Complete applications |
| 3. Interview | Video call with all family members | 2 weeks | Interviewed families |
| 4. Reference Check | Professional, personal, outdoor references | 2 weeks | Verified families |
| 5. Skills Assessment | Wilderness skills evaluation | 1 week | Assessed families |
| 6. Offer & Commitment | Financial commitment, contracts | 4 weeks | Committed families |
| 7. Pre-Training | 2-4 week preparation program | 4 weeks | Launch-ready families |

### Selection Criteria Scoring

| Criterion | Weight | Scoring Method |
|-----------|--------|----------------|
| Wilderness readiness | 20% | Skills assessment, experience |
| Family cohesion | 20% | Interview observation |
| Community fit | 20% | Values alignment, references |
| Educational commitment | 15% | Homeschool history, goals |
| Financial stability | 15% | Documentation, reserve funds |
| Health/safety | 10% | Medical questionnaire, disclosures |

### Red Flags (Automatic Disqualification)

- History of community conflict or litigation
- Untreated mental health conditions affecting safety
- Criminal record involving violence or children
- Inability to commit full year
- Single-income family without remote work capability
- Expecting child with due date during expedition

## Community Governance Design

### NASA Mission Role Structure

| Role | Primary | Backup | Responsibility |
|------|---------|--------|----------------|
| Medical Lead | Family A adult | Family B adult | First response, health monitoring |
| Safety Officer | Family C adult | Family D adult | Risk assessment, emergency protocols |
| Water/Sanitation Lead | Family E adult | Family F adult | Infrastructure operations |
| Communications Lead | Family G adult | Family H adult | External contact, tech systems |
| Education Coordinator | Family I adult | Family J adult | Curriculum, citizen science |
| Logistics Coordinator | Family K adult | Family L adult | Supplies, inventory |
| Community Facilitator | Family M adult | Family N adult | Meetings, conflict resolution |
| Youth Council Rep | Rotating teen | Rotating teen | Youth voice in governance |

### Decision-Making Framework

| Decision Type | Who Decides | Method | Timeline |
|---------------|-------------|--------|----------|
| Daily Operations | Role leads | Individual/pair | Immediate |
| Weekly Logistics | Operations group | Quick consensus | 24 hours |
| Community Policies | All adults | Full consensus | 1 week |
| Safety/Emergency | Safety Officer | Authority | Immediate |
| Financial (small) | Role lead + Finance | Approval | 48 hours |
| Financial (large) | All adults | Full consensus | 1 week |
| Conflict Resolution | Facilitator + parties | Mediated process | 3-7 days |
| Family Departure | Mission Control + family | Structured process | 2 weeks |

### Conflict Resolution Protocol

| Level | Trigger | Process | Escalation |
|-------|---------|---------|------------|
| 1. Informal | Minor friction | Direct conversation | If unresolved >24h |
| 2. Facilitated | Ongoing tension | Facilitator-led dialogue | If unresolved >1 week |
| 3. Community | Affects community | Community circle, all voices | If unresolved >2 weeks |
| 4. External | Serious conflict | External mediator (remote) | If unresolved >1 month |
| 5. Departure | Irreconcilable | Structured departure process | Final option |

## Family Support Systems

| Support Type | Frequency | Method |
